Singapore Sling

Shirley temple vibes

Servings: 1

Time: 5m

Ingredients:

  • ½ oz. lime juice
  • 1 oz. pineapple juice
  • ½ oz. maraschino cherry juice
  • 1 tsp. grenadine
  • club soda or ginger beer
  • Cherries and sliced limes for serving

Steps:

  1. Combine all ingredients except soda in a shaker with ice and shake or stir. Pour into a tall Collins glass and top with soda. Garnish with a lime wheel and cherry.
