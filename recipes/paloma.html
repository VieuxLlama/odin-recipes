Paloma

Tasty grapefruit lime drink

Servings: 1

Time: 5m

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz grapefruit juice
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz agave syrup
  • Sparkling water
  • Ice
  • Sliced limes and grapefruit, for serving

Steps:

  1. Add the grapefruit juice, lime juice, and maple syrup to a glass and stir until well combined.
  2. Fill the glass with ice.
  3. Top of the glass with sparkling water, stir to combine, and serve with a slice of lime and fresh grapefruit.
