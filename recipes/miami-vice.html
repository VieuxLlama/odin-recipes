Miami Vice

Strawberries at the BEACH

Servings: 2

Time: 20m

Ingredients:

Strawberry Daiquiri Layer:

  • 1 cup Strawberries chopped
  • 1 oz fresh Lime Juice
  • ½ oz Simple Syrup
  • 1 cup Ice

Piña Colada Layer:

  • 2 oz Coconut Cream
  • 2 oz Pineapple Juice
  • 1 cup Ice

Steps:

  1. Blend together all the strawberry daiquiri ingredients and pour into glasses
  2. Place glasses in the freezer while making the next layer
  3. Rinse the blender
  4. Blend together all of the piña colada ingredients and pour on top of the strawberry layer (pour over a spoon so the layers don’t mix)
  5. All done yay

Notes:

  • Fresh limes and strawberries are worth it bro just get the good stuff
  • Could always just blend everything together instead of doing the layers if you wanna be boring
